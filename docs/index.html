Biggest Upsets

Biggest Carry Jobs

Most Balanced Wins

Lowest coefficient of variation (std ÷ mean) across the starting lineup — every slot contributed. Wins only, minimum 100 total pts to filter out "everyone was equally bad" blowouts.

Most Efficient Wins

Team-wide fantasy points per touch. Offensive pts = starter pts from QB/RB/WR/TE (K and DEF don't have touches, so they're excluded from both sides of the ratio). Touches sum across the offense: rush attempts + receptions, plus QB pass attempts. Wins only. Historical seasons use ESPN's NFL box-score API joined to the NFL.com fantasy data by player name.

GOAT Teams

All-time best team-seasons, scored out of 100: PPG (25), all-play win % (25), start/sit efficiency (20), elite players rostered — top 10 at each position (15), and a +15 championship bonus. In-progress seasons excluded.

Winning Players

Players ranked by championship-roster appearances and win % when they were in the starting lineup. Only players with 5+ career starts qualify. Click a player to open their page.

Mia Khalifa's

Players passed around the most — ranked by number of unique managers who've ever rostered them. Also shows ownership transitions (every owner change counts).

Worst Drops OAT

The ones that got away. For each (year, player) we find the biggest drop regret — pts the player went on to score as a starter for OTHER owners the rest of that season after being cut. Trades are excluded.

Playoff Odds

Monte Carlo projection for the in-progress season. Each team's remaining weeks are rolled from their season-to-date scoring distribution (mean + std) against the real schedule, 10,000 simulations. Top 4 by W-L (ties broken on PF) make the playoffs — probabilities sum to 400%.

The One Start Club

Players who were started exactly once by any manager in league history. Ranked by points in that one and only start — the biggest cup-of-coffee legends. Click a row to open the game.

Player History

Game History

Pick two managers (and optionally a season / week) to pull up any matchup's full lineup breakdown. Clicking any linked game anywhere on the dashboard also opens it here.

Trade History

Reconstructed by diffing weekly rosters: when players move both ways between two teams across consecutive weeks, we flag it as a trade. High-confidence = detected count ≤ official season trade count for both sides; low-confidence = we may be picking up coincident waiver churn. Score is a concentration-weighted sum of post-trade starter points (top player 100%, next 50%, next 25%…) so a lone superstar beats a bundle of role players. Raw total pts received shown in muted text.
